NIX / WHY NIX?

Your computer should begin with you.

A new way to think about computing: intent first, intelligence on your machine, and control in your hands.

WHY NIX? / A DIFFERENT STARTING POINT

You built the computer.
Now make it feel like yours.

You chose the parts. You put in the time. Your operating system should meet that same ambition. NIX starts with a simple question: what do you want to do?

01

Intent before interface.

Start with “I want to write a book,” not a hunt through applications and menus. The NIX vision is to bring the right workspace to the task.

02

Intelligence close to home.

The architecture puts model inference on your computer. Network research and downloads are separate choices with separate permissions.

03

You keep the final say.

An assistant can suggest a plan. Permission checks and trusted services decide what can execute. Your files and devices do not become open territory for an agent.

YOUR COMPUTER / YOUR CONTROL

NIX is not…

01 / LINUXNot Linux.

02 / WINDOWSNot Windows.

03 / APPLENot macOS.

And yet, the goal is to run the software you know.

A new foundation shouldn’t mean leaving every familiar tool behind. NIX proposes native applications, compatibility runtimes, and isolated Windows or Linux guest Pods—chosen around what you want to do. You choose the task. You approve the permissions. You stay in charge.

Instead of attempting to “control” your computer…
we’re building an operating system that gives YOU control.

NIX is in development. Application compatibility depends on the runtime, hardware, and testing; macOS and Apple application support is not currently proposed. Explore the Pod architecture ↗

CHOICE / WITHOUT THE RITUAL

A prompt is the beginning.
You choose the rest.

A desktop can be useful. So can a terminal. NIX makes both available by request, while keeping the prompt as the starting point. The goal is a computer that adapts to your work without asking you to adopt a single way of working.
